✦ Zero Data Collection

Asma collects zero personal data. It transmits zero data to any server. It has no analytics, no advertising network, and no remote backend of any kind. Everything you do in Asma stays on your device — forever.

Last Updated: June 7, 2026  ·  Publisher: RidgeField Apps  ·  Contact: ridgefieldapps@gmail.com

1. Data We Collect

We collect nothing.

Asma is engineered as a 100% offline-first application. All content, databases, and computations operate entirely on your local device. No personal information, usage data, behavioral analytics, location data, or spiritual content is ever collected by RidgeField Apps.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data exists only on your device and is never transmitted:

Data Type Storage Accessible to RidgeField Apps?
Tasbih counter sessions SQLite (local) Never
Journal entries (Muhasabah) Encrypted via hardware-backed secure store Never
Takhalluq challenge progress SQLite (local) Never
Name favorites & recitation counts SQLite (local) Never
Settings and preferences AsyncStorage (local) Never
Notification schedules Device OS (local) Never

3. The Muhasabah Journal

Hardware-Backed Encryption

The premium Muhasabah Journal uses hardware-backed encryption via your device's secure enclave or Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). Journal entries are encrypted with biometric-backed keys before being written to device storage. Neither RidgeField Apps, Google, nor any third party has the technical means to access this content.

4. Location Data (Qibla Feature)

On-Device Only, Never Stored

The Qibla direction feature requests one-time device location access to compute the great-circle bearing to Mecca. This computation is performed entirely on-device using spherical trigonometry. Your location coordinates are never transmitted to any server, never stored beyond the current session, and never logged or retained.

5. Notifications

Local Only — No Push Servers

All notifications in Asma are local notifications scheduled by the device OS. No push notification servers, FCM tokens, or APNS registration occurs. Turning off notifications in system settings fully disables all notification activity.

6. Audio

Bundled in the App

All Arabic pronunciation audio files are stored within the app bundle and played locally via expo-av. No audio is streamed, recorded, or transmitted.

7. In-App Purchases (Premium Features)

Handled by Google Play Billing

If you purchase the premium tier, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. RidgeField Apps receives only the anonymous purchase confirmation token from the OS. No payment card data, personal account information, or purchase history is stored by Asma. "Restore Purchases" works by querying your Google Play account directly via native OS hooks — no Asma server is involved.

8. Third-Party Services

None.

Asma contains no third-party SDKs for analytics (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), advertising (AdMob, Meta Audience Network, etc.), crash reporting (Sentry, Crashlytics, etc.), or remote configuration. The app has no internet permission requirements for its core functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Asma does not collect any data from any user. As such, it is safe for use by individuals of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or process any data from children or adults.

10. Data Deletion

Because no data is ever sent to our servers, there is nothing to delete remotely. All local device data can be cleared via:

  • Settings → Clear All Local Data (within the app)
  • Uninstalling the application (Android will clear all app data)

11. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and notify users through the app's settings screen.
